# Untitled

A fast paced, easy to pickup and learn, FPS shooter. Decent realism but more focus on gameplay and action, fun gunplay and intricate depth to customization.

Inspired by Modern Warfare 2 and the original COD4. Focus on multiplayer initially, LAN party compatible (so easy to setup a local server). For maps I want them to be inspired by the original COD4, MW and MW2 maps. Guns similarly. Killstreaks and customizability in your loadouts with perks, weapon customization etc that can be unlocekd through a fun mechanism.

Gamemodes from Team vs. Team deathmatch, FFA, bomb planting and defusal, headquarters, zone control and let's come up with some creative gamemodes ourselves too. 

The definition for a MVP would be Team deathmatch, 3-5 weapons with a different sight and silencer for example, 2 maps. LAN server and possibilities for lobby management (online and local).

## Decisions so far

- **Goal:** prove it's fun at an upcoming LAN party, less than 1 month away. Solo dev. A public release is undecided, so don't build for it yet.
- **Working mode (2026-10-27):** get working demos of every feature first, as fast and token-cheap as possible. Verification is a quick smoke pass; deeper testing, cleanup and polish come later, once things work.
- **Stack:** Rust, Bevy + lightyear (netcode) + avian3d (physics) + bevy_trenchbroom (TrenchBroom brush maps). Pin all versions on day 1 and don't upgrade before the party. Targets: Linux/macOS/Windows.
- **Topology:** one binary. "Host Game" runs a listen server; a `--dedicated` flag runs it headless. LAN UDP-broadcast **Server Browser** plus join-by-IP. Online lobby/relay is deferred.
- **Authority:** clients own their own movement, and the server decides hits (it raycasts each shot and applies damage). No prediction or lag compensation until we play over the internet. Anti-cheat is the honor system.
- **Lifecycle:** CoD-style. Players gather in a **Lobby**, then **Matches** cycle through the **Rotation**. **Join In Progress** auto-balances to the smaller team.
- **Modes:** Team Deathmatch + Domination. Domination gets cut first if gunfeel isn't fun by the end of week 2.
- **Player count:** design for 6–10 (3v3–5v5). Later target is 24–32, so Game Modes, Maps and Lobby sizes will need per-size tuning (e.g. max players per Map/Mode). Noted, not built yet.
- **Maps:** the party gets 2 **Homage Maps**, Backlot and Vacant (CoD4). They stay private: never in a public build or repo, because Activision DMCA'd CoD map ports in Oct 2026. Any public release uses **Remix Maps** instead.
- **Art:** CC0 low-poly (Quaternius/Kenney models, ambientCG/Poly Haven textures). First-person weapon animation is procedural (recoil, sway, ADS). Team colour comes from a tint. Audio is CC0 or cheap packs.
- **Weapons:** AR, SMG, Sniper, Shotgun as **Primaries**, pistol as **Secondary**. All hitscan. Stats are data-driven and can be tuned without recompiling.
- **Bolt-action snipers:** firing keeps you scoped. The next round isn't chambered until you rack the bolt: while scoped, a click racks it (click racks only, the next click fires; slow, ~1.4 s, still zoomed with some sway). Releasing the scope racks automatically at the faster unscoped rate (~0.9 s), and releasing mid-rack switches the rest to the faster rate. You can't fire until the bolt is closed. Tunables per Weapon in weapons.ron.
- **Future: bullet travel time.** Once the larger Maps land, long-range Weapons (snipers first, maybe all) switch from hitscan to simulated projectiles with travel time and drop, simulated on the server. Players will need to lead and hold over targets at range, which is why click-to-rack while scoped matters. Wind (see weather) can be layered on later. This changes the hit model in ADR 0002, so it gets its own ADR when built.
- **Aim mechanics (all Weapons, tuned per Weapon in weapons.ron):**
  - **Idle sway:** a slow figure-eight drift while aiming down sights. Strong on snipers, mild on ARs, minimal on SMGs and pistols; reduced while crouched or prone.
  - **Flinch:** taking a hit kicks the view (pitch/yaw), scaled per Weapon and reduced by ADS.
  - **Hold breath** (Shift while aiming): steadies sway for ~4 s, then sway is worse for a moment. Only on Weapons or Optics that allow it (snipers, ACOG).
  - **Scope glint:** other players see a glare from scoped snipers and ACOGs aimed toward them.
  - **Projectile snipers:** sniper bullets become server-simulated projectiles with game-tuned speed (~550–650 m/s) and gravity, so drop shows only past ~80 m. ARs, SMGs and pistols stay hitscan. Comes after the bigger Maps land, with an ADR.
  - **Optics by magnification:** one number per optic drives field of view, sway visibility, hold-breath and glint (both unlock at ≥ 3×). Each optic also has a small `sway_scale`. Lineup: iron sights 1×, Red Dot 1×, Holographic 1.5×, Prism 2×, ACOG 4×, sniper scope 8×.
- **Class points ("Pick 10", Black Ops 2 style):** every class has a 10-point budget.
  - Costs: Primary 1, Secondary 1, each Attachment 1 (4×+ optics and the grenade launcher cost 2), each Perk 1, each piece of equipment (lethal/tactical) 1.
  - **Wildcards** (1 point each) break the slot rules: Perk Greed (a second Perk in one slot), Gunfighter (a third Primary attachment), Overkill (two Primaries).
  - Dropping things frees points: e.g. no Secondary means one more Perk.
- **Two unlock tracks:** your account **Level** unlocks Weapons, Perks, equipment and Wildcards. Each Weapon's own level, from kills with it, unlocks that Weapon's Attachments, then its camo challenges.
- **Equipment:** lethal frag grenade; tactical flashbang and smoke. Grenades are physical projectiles (bounce, fuse).
- **M16A4:** a 3-round burst rifle. It is the only Weapon that takes the underslung grenade launcher (the "noob tube", 2 points, physical arcing projectile).
- **Fire modes:** X cycles the Weapon's fire modes (B already opens the Loadout screen). Each Weapon lists its modes in weapons.ron: Auto, Burst(n) with its own burst rate and a delay between bursts, Semi, or Bolt. Examples: M4 Auto/Burst 3/Semi, M16A4 Burst 3/Semi, MP5 Auto/Burst 2/Semi, pistol Semi, sniper Bolt. The HUD shows the current mode next to the ammo, the switch makes a click sound, and the last mode used is remembered per Weapon in the Profile. Shots still reach the server one by one, so the server needs no change.
- **Manual reload:** an empty magazine doesn't reload on its own. Pulling the trigger on empty gives a dry-fire click (each pull, no auto-repeat) and a flashing "RELOAD" hint, and R reloads. A Gameplay setting "Auto reload" (default off) brings the old behaviour back for players who want it.
- **Levels shown to others:** the Scoreboard shows each player's Level and a rank insignia before their name. Insignia are generated per Level band, like the emblems (chevrons → bars → stars). Levels live in each client's Profile, so the client reports its Level on join and on level-up (trusted, ADR 0002) and the server replicates it. A level-up is announced to everyone as a system line in chat.
- **Text chat:** Y = all, U = team. A small fading chat box bottom-left also carries system lines (level-ups, joins/leaves, team switches).
- **Audio pass (short, keep it simple):**
  - **Rear cue test:** Bevy's spatial audio is plain stereo panning, so a sound behind you is indistinguishable from one in front. Try a small spatializer of our own: low-pass the sound and drop it ~3 dB as it moves behind the listener, with stronger left/right panning. Drop it if it doesn't help in a playtest.
  - **Footsteps:** remote footsteps step every 0.85 m whatever the speed (5.9 steps/s walking, 9.4 sprinting) and play a bullet impact slowed to ~0.6×, so a sprint sounds like a rumble. Step about 2.2/s walking rising to ~3/s sprinting, from the smoothed replicated speed, and use real footstep recordings (Kenney "Impact Sounds", CC0, `footstep_concrete_000`–`004`, random pick, ±5% pitch).
- **Settings tabs:**
  - **Video:** display mode (windowed / borderless fullscreen / exclusive fullscreen), resolution (from the monitor's modes), vsync, FPS cap, render scale, a quality preset (Low/Medium/High/Custom) for shadows, ambient occlusion, bloom and anti-aliasing, and brightness. Display changes apply live, with a 10 s "Keep these settings?" revert.
  - **Audio:** master, effects, music and voice volumes.
  - **Controls:** sensitivity, ADS sensitivity multiplier, invert Y, and a key-rebinding list.
  - **Gameplay:** name, FOV, and the CoD4 texture path.
  All saved in settings.ron.
- **Create a Class, MW2 style:** reachable from the main menu (CREATE A CLASS) as well as in game. Three levels deep, never a scrolling form:
  1. **Class list:** the default + custom classes on the left; the selected class's summary on the right.
  2. **Slot overview:** big tiles for Primary (with its attachments), Secondary, Lethal, Tactical, Perk 1–3 and Wildcards, plus the points meter. Each tile shows the current pick, its icon and its cost.
  3. **Picker:** clicking a tile opens a full-screen picker for that slot. A list on the left (locked items greyed, with their unlock), and on the right a preview with stat bars, description and cost. Confirm returns to the overview.
  Weapons open a sub-picker for attachments and camo. Keyboard and mouse both work: arrows/Enter/Esc.
- **Gunplay rules (per Weapon):** separate hip and ADS fire rates (ADS ~5% slower for most guns, equal for SMGs). Sprint-out delay before firing/aiming. Hip spread blooms while moving and more while airborne. Crouch/prone tighten spread and recoil. Weapon weight scales move speed.
- **Hit Zones:** head, upper torso, lower torso and limbs, each with its own hitbox. Damage multipliers are set per Weapon (e.g. AR head 1.4, limbs 0.8; sniper upper torso 1.2, so chest shots one-shot and limb shots don't).
- **Death presentation:** on death, a client-side body tips over and stays ~10 s, then fades (cosmetic, no hitbox). A respawn teleports with no visible glide; the player stays hidden until they're at the spawn.
- **HUD in Match Rules:** each preset has a `hud` block (crosshair, hitmarkers, red kill hitmarker, kill feed, damage direction, later minimap). The server tells clients the active preset; personal settings can only hide more. `kill_feed_delay_secs` lets the kill feed show kills 5–10 s late (Rising Storm style). A **Hardcore** preset ships: 30 HP, no regen, no crosshair or hitmarkers, and a delayed kill feed.
- **Maps (format):** TrenchBroom `.map` files loaded with bevy_trenchbroom. The first versions are generated from scripted layouts and stay editable in TrenchBroom. Map entities carry spawn points per Team, Domination Flags, HQ sites and S&D bomb sites.
- **Look per Map ("bright but gritty"):** surfaces keep saturated colours that pop, but every Map has its own look preset (grade, fog, sun, sky, exposure) layered on top to make it darker and grittier. Examples: overcast grey for urban maps, sepia/yellow dust for desert maps, a cold blue dawn for a winter map. Presets live in data and a Map picks one.
- **Future: weather and wind.** Hazy desert looks get blowing dust/sand particles driven by a wind direction and strength. Wind could be randomised per map load and could push long-range shots once Weapons have real projectiles (today all shots are hitscan). Undecided: whether wind affects only snipers or all bullets, and how it's shown to players.
- **Factions per Map theater:** each Map names the two Factions that fight on it (desert, Eastern European winter, port city...), and later each Faction gets its own character models. Nine are in `data/factions.ron` (Task Force Ranger, Harmattan Legion, Northern Sabre, Volkov Battalion, Baekho Recon, Tianlang Group, Simba Brigade, Steppe Wolves, Crimson Vanguard); their emblems are worn cloth patches made by `tools/emblems/emblems.py`.
- **Homage Maps distribution:** a second private repo, `frontline-private`, cloned into `content/private/` (git-ignored here). See ADR 0003.
- **Next Game Modes:** Domination, Search & Destroy, Headquarters, plus a **Rotation** with Host Map/Mode selection.
- **Attachments:** two **Attachment Slots**, Optic (Red Dot, ACOG) and Muzzle (Silencer).
- **Progression:** party-compressed XP **Levels** (~10 in an evening) unlock **Weapons**, **Attachments** and **Perks**.
- **Perks:** 3 **Perk Slots** × 2 simple stat modifiers.
- **Killstreaks:** fixed **UAV** (3 kills) + **Airstrike** (5 kills). This needs a **Minimap** and a map-overview target picker.
- **Cut order** if behind schedule, first cut first: Levels (everything unlocked) → Airstrike → Domination → Backlot → Perks. Never cut: movement/gunfeel, LAN join, Team Deathmatch, Vacant, the 5 Weapons.
- **Time:** full-time (~35+ h/week). Gate: by the end of week 1, two machines on LAN are moving and shooting each other in a greybox. If that slips more than 3 days, apply cuts 1–3 immediately.
- **Fire Pings:** unsuppressed shots show on enemies' **Minimap** for ~1.5s. The Silencer removes the ping in exchange for less range.
- **Profile:** client-local file (name, XP, Level) sent on join. The server awards XP and the client saves it. Survives a change of Host.
- **Health/TTK default:** CoD core. 100 HP, regen after ~5s, AR kills in 3–4 body shots up close, ~1.4× headshots, damage falls off with range, 3s respawn, spawns avoid enemy line of sight. All of these are **Match Rules** values, not hardcoded. Each Game Mode has default Match Rules. **Hardcore** and health-pack variants are presets added later.
- **Movement:** walk, sprint (limited; Marathon extends it), crouch, prone, jump, **Mantle** (ledges up to ~1.6 m, ~0.4 s). Landing briefly slows you instead of sliding. ADS slows you. No slide or lean. Mantle reverses the earlier "no mantle" call (2026-10-26) to make 1–1.5 m cover climbable. All numbers in `data/movement.ron`.
- **Dive to prone:** going prone while sprinting dives forward into prone: a short lunge (~4 m/s for ~0.35 s) that keeps some momentum and drops you behind cover, then a brief recovery (~0.5 s: no moving, firing or standing, looking is fine) and a short get-up wait before you can stand or sprint. It must slow you overall, so run/prone/run spamming isn't faster than running. The weapon is lowered throughout. Tunables are in movement.ron.
- **Host setup screen:** HOST GAME opens a setup screen before starting. It picks the **Rotation** (Map + Game Mode + Match Rules preset per entry; a single entry is a plain one-map game), server name, max players, and the per-mode score/time limits. Settings are remembered between sessions.
- **Custom Match Rules:** the Host can create named rule sets in a rules editor: max health, regen, respawn delay, damage scale, friendly fire, fall damage, and the HUD toggles. Built-in presets (Default, Hardcore) are read-only starting points to copy. Custom sets are saved locally and are selectable per Rotation entry. The server sends the full rule values to clients, so clients don't need the preset file.
- **Parked: fun MW2-twist modes** (to debate later). Candidates: Kill Confirmed, Gun Game, Infected, Juggernaut, Demolition.
- **Map scale (2026-10-27):** enlarge Maps rather than shrink players. Players stay real-world scale (1.8 m, walk 5.5 m/s) so props, doors and cars keep their real sizes. Size target is the time to cross the map at walking pace: about 15 s for small maps (Vacant-style) and 20–25 s for medium ones (Backlot, Crossroads, Arena). Objectives keep a minimum spacing: HQ sites about 30 m apart, Dom Flags about 25 m, bomb sites about 30 m. Fog visibility is retuned per look to the new sizes. Done by spreading the layouts (buildings and props keep their size, the gaps, yards and lanes grow and get more cover); `tools/mapgen/kit.py` measures the longest spawn-to-spawn walk and enforces the spacing when a Map is generated. Measured longest Alpha-Bravo walk, before to after: Arena 75 to 115 m, Crossroads 74 to 130 m, Backlot 106 to 119 m, Vacant 100 to 112 m (Vacant was already above its 15 s target, so it only grew a little, enough for 30 m HQ spacing).
- **Input:** keyboard + mouse only, with rebindable keys and a sensitivity setting. No controller. Set up a 3-OS CI build matrix (Linux/macOS/Windows) in week 1.
- **Teams:** free pick, including on **Join In Progress** (the smaller team is pre-selected). Stacking is allowed. Friendly fire is a **Match Rules** option, off by default.
- **Loadouts:** the **Profile** holds 3 custom and 3 default (AR/SMG/Sniper preset) **Loadouts**. They're editable in the menu or mid-Match. A change applies on the next spawn, or instantly within ~5s of spawning. The server trusts the Loadout and checks it against Level unlocks only loosely.
- **Domination:** CoD4 defaults. Solo capture takes ~10s; more teammates capture faster (capped at 3×). When both teams are on a Flag it's contested and progress freezes. An enemy Flag must be neutralized before you can capture it. +1 point per held Flag every 5s. Win at 200 points or 10 min. Spawns are biased toward held Flags. All values are data-driven.
- **XP (starting values, all configurable):** kill 100, headshot +50, Flag capture 150, Flag-defend kill +50, Match win 500, Match played 200. An **Assist** scales with damage dealt (e.g. ~1 XP per HP, so 99 HP ≈ 99). **Multikill** bonuses: double +50, triple +100, and so on. Level n needs ~1500×n. Unlocks: L1 AR/SMG/Sniper + Red Dot + 1 Perk per slot; L2 custom Loadouts; L3 Shotgun; L4 ACOG; L5 Silencer; L6–8 second Perks.
- **Killstreak chaining:** MW2 rule. Kills from a **Killstreak** count toward the next one. Airstrike team damage follows the friendly-fire **Match Rules** setting.
- **Airstrike targeting:** a full-screen top-down Map overview, click a point, and the strike lands ~3s later. The player stays vulnerable while the overview is open.
- **Platforms:** Windows, Linux, macOS are all confirmed at the party. CI builds all three.
- **Codename:** `frontline` (crate/binary name). Replaces the `mw2` folder name before anything goes public.
- **Deadline relaxed (2026-10-26):** build as far as we get; the party isn't a hard scope gate. The cut order now just sets priority.
- **Playtest feedback (2026-10-27):** reloads from an empty magazine take longer than tactical ones (`empty_reload_secs` per Weapon; Sleight of Hand x0.7). Stairs are ramps and are walked at full horizontal speed; bodies also step up 0.45 m, taller cover is a jump or **Mantle**. **Fall damage** is a **Match Rules** value (`fall_damage`: none below `min_height`, lethal at `max_height`, counted from the peak of the fall); the client reports its landing and the server applies it with no attacker. Dead players can ask to respawn early after `min_respawn_secs` (Space or click; the normal delay stays the maximum), and the pause menu's RESPAWN kills a stuck player with no credit (not in Search & Destroy). Switching Teams skips the Loadout screen so the death screen shows; in Search & Destroy the switcher waits for the next round.
